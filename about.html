About ShiftMate

ShiftMate Australia is an independent set of free browser-based tools designed to make everyday shift and gig-work calculations easier.

What we are building

The goal is simple calculators first, followed by useful guides and optional downloadable tools. ShiftMate is not affiliated with Fair Work, the Australian Taxation Office, an employer, payroll provider or delivery platform.

Corrections

Rates and rules change. The calculators therefore rely primarily on figures entered by the user rather than claiming to determine an award entitlement automatically.
