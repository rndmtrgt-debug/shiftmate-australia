What to record for each shift

Add the date, paid hours, hourly rate and any tips you want included. TORVILO calculates a total for each row and a combined summary, giving you a simple personal record to compare with other work information.

Where your shift records are stored

Your tracker entries are saved in local browser storage on the device and browser you are using. They are not an online account or cloud backup, so clearing browser data, changing devices or using another browser can make those locally stored records unavailable.

Export a backup

Use Export CSV to download a copy of the tracker records. Keeping periodic exports can give you a separate record that can be opened in spreadsheet software.

Useful next steps

Use the Shift Pay Calculator to estimate a shift before recording it, or the Paid Hours & Break Calculator to work out paid hours. When comparing your records with a payment, see How to check your pay and Understanding Australian pay rates.
