Annual Salary to Hourly Rate Calculator

Convert an annual salary into an equivalent hourly rate and gross pay figures.

Enter your salary and working pattern to calculate.
Estimate only: This is a gross-pay conversion. It does not determine award rates, overtime, allowances, tax, super or whether a salary package includes super.

How the conversion works

The annual salary is divided by the paid weeks you enter and then by your paid hours per week. This gives an equivalent gross hourly rate for comparing different working patterns.
