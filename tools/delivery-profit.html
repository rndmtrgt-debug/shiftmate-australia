How the delivery profit calculator works

Enter your delivery income, fuel cost, other session costs, hours online and kilometres driven. TORVILO subtracts the entered costs from income and shows estimated operating profit, profit per hour and profit per kilometre.

Why track more than delivery income?

Gross platform income does not show what remains after operating costs. Recording both income and costs can give you a clearer picture of how an individual delivery session performed.

What can count as other costs?

The other-costs field can be used for session costs you want included in your own profitability calculation. It is not a tax-deduction calculator, and an expense being included here does not mean it is deductible for tax purposes.
