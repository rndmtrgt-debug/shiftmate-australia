How the income target calculator works

Enter the gross income target you want to model, your hourly rate and a rate multiplier. TORVILO divides the target by the effective hourly rate to estimate how many paid hours would be needed to reach that amount.

Planning with different rates

You can change the hourly rate or multiplier to compare scenarios. A multiplier of 1 uses the entered hourly rate as-is, while a higher multiplier models a higher effective rate.

Useful next steps

To estimate one specific shift, use the Shift Pay Calculator. To record hours you have actually worked, use the Shift Tracker. You can also read Understanding Australian pay rates before choosing a rate to enter.

About the result

The result is a planning estimate based only on the target, rate and multiplier you provide. It does not account for unpaid breaks, changing rates across a period, tax, allowances or other payroll adjustments.
