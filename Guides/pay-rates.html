WORKER GUIDE

Understanding Australian pay rates

Your legal minimum is not always one universal hourly number.

Awards and agreements

Most Australian employees are covered by a modern award or registered agreement. The applicable minimum rate can depend on the instrument covering the job and the employee’s classification.

Penalty rates

Higher rates can apply for work at particular times, including weekends, public holidays, overtime, late nights or early mornings. The applicable rules depend on the award or agreement.

Why ShiftMate asks you for a rate

Rather than pretending to identify every worker’s legal rate, ShiftMate’s calculator lets you enter a rate you have verified. This keeps the calculator useful even when awards change.

Current context (September 2026): Fair Work states the National Minimum Wage for award/agreement-free adults is $26.44 per hour from 1 July 2026. Award-covered employees may have different and higher minimums. Check the current official rate for your circumstances.

Useful official checks

Use the Fair Work Ombudsman Pay and Conditions Tool and current pay guides to verify the award, classification, penalties and allowances that apply.
