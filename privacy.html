Privacy

TORVILO is designed to provide useful browser-based tools while keeping data collection limited.

Calculator information

The core calculators run in your browser and do not require an account. Figures entered into the calculators are used to perform the requested calculation.

Shift Tracker storage

Shift Tracker entries are stored in local browser storage on the device and browser you are using. They are not a TORVILO cloud account or backup. Clearing browser data, changing devices or using another browser can make those locally stored records unavailable.

Current third-party services

TORVILO includes Google AdSense site code for site verification, review and advertising services. TORVILO does not currently require a user account or use a TORVILO contact form. Google and hosting providers may process technical information as described by their own policies and applicable consent choices. The website is hosted using third-party web infrastructure, which may process ordinary technical request information required to deliver the site.

Planned advertising and cookies

TORVILO uses Google AdSense site code and has configured Google's consent-management platform. Advertising availability and display depend on Google's review, settings and applicable consent choices. If Google advertising is enabled, Google and other applicable advertising vendors may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, measure and personalise advertising in accordance with their policies and user choices.

Users can manage personalised advertising through Google Ads Settings. Where consent is required, TORVILO will use an appropriate consent-management process before applicable advertising technologies are used.

External links

TORVILO links to external websites, including official government information. Those websites have their own privacy practices and are responsible for their own content and data handling.

Contact

For privacy questions or corrections, see Contact TORVILO. Do not include sensitive personal information in a public message.
