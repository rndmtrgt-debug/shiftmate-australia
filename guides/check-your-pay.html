How to check your pay

TORVILO can help with arithmetic and record keeping, but your correct minimum rate depends on your employment circumstances.

1. Keep your shift details

Record dates, start and finish times, unpaid breaks and what you were paid.

2. Identify what covers your work

Your rate can depend on an award, enterprise agreement, classification and employment type.

3. Check official information

Use the Fair Work Ombudsman minimum wages information and official pay guides to check current minimum pay information.

4. Compare like with like

Compare the same pay period and distinguish ordinary hours, penalties, overtime and allowances.

Awards and rates can change. Always verify current official information.
