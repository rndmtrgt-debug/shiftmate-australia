WORKER GUIDE

Track what a delivery session really earns

Revenue alone does not tell you whether a session was worthwhile.

Keep a simple session record

Record gross platform income, tips or bonuses, hours online, kilometres driven, fuel and other directly related costs.

Tax records are separate

Tax treatment depends on your circumstances. Keep supporting records and use current Australian Taxation Office guidance. Generally, records should support the income and expenses you report and help show how you worked out any deduction claimed.

See the ATO record-keeping guidance for current official information.
